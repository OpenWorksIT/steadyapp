## How a paper gets here

1. **It starts in the community.** The author posts it in the Steady Community, the private forum for clinicians who use Steady, under Research and studies.
2. **Clinicians read it and comment**, for at least two weeks: the method, the claims, the references, what it means in practice. The author answers and revises.
3. **When the author is ready**, OpenWorks IT checks the references and links, that no patient can be identified, that any study with patients' data names its ethics approval, and that every questionnaire it reproduces may be reproduced.
4. **It's published**: on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/), CERN's open research repository, in the Steady community there, which gives it a DOI that won't change, and here, with the PDF and the full text.

## What "discussed in the community" means

The clinicians who comment are colleagues, not a journal's appointed reviewers, and their comments stay in the private forum. It is not journal peer review. Read a paper here as you would a preprint: carefully, with its references.

## Corrections and new versions

A correction or an update is a new version: Zenodo keeps every version, and the DOI on each page always leads to the latest. Each page says which version it shows.

## Reusing a paper

Every paper is published under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): you may share and adapt it for any purpose, with credit to its authors and a link to it. Questionnaires quoted in a paper keep their own licences.

## For authors

If you use Steady in your practice, you're already a member of the community: post your paper there. Questions about publishing: [info@openworks.co.za](mailto:info@openworks.co.za).
