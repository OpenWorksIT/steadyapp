## What you see

Your home screen is your patient list. Patients with something worth a look come first: an unreviewed flag, a missed run of check-ins, or a finished questionnaire. Open a patient and you see their last fortnight as a line, their own words from each check-in, their questionnaire scores over time and, if they've chosen to share it, a daily summary from their watch. RADR shows how far a patient's watch data and check-ins are from that person's own normal, with every number explained. [More on RADR](/science/#radr).

Flags are review aids, not risk scores. Steady highlights PHQ-9 item 9, low mood in three check-ins in a row, high scores and missed check-ins, so you can decide what they mean. You mark a flag as reviewed when you've looked at it.

Steady doesn't diagnose, doesn't predict, and isn't watched around the clock. Every patient screen says so and shows crisis numbers.

## Questionnaires and exercises

Built in: **PHQ-9, GAD-7, PCL-5 and WHO-5**, and five exercises: box breathing, 4-7-8 breathing, slow paced breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding and a thought record. Send any of them once, daily or weekly, with a note such as "Before Thursday's session, please."

You can also:

- **Write your own** questionnaires and exercises in the app, with your own answer scale, score bands and review flags.
- **Import** from the open ReproSchema library (more than 90 validated instruments), from a FHIR Questionnaire file, or from a Steady file a colleague shares.

Every instrument carries a licence status. Imported ones wait as "Check licence" until you confirm your practice may use them, so nothing goes to a patient by accident. [More on the science](/science/).

## Your notifications

You get a notification when a patient checks in or finishes something you sent. It says only that there's something new. Names and health details stay inside the app, behind your sign-in.

## Exports for the clinical file

Export a patient's report, with charts, to PDF, or one or all of your patients to Excel. For AI note-taking tools there's a plain-text PDF with only what's new since the last one, so each file adds to the record without repeating it. Every export says how it was made and whether each flag was reviewed. The clinical record stays yours. Steady is a between-session tool, not your file.

## Your server

Each practice gets its own Steady server, hosted in South Africa:

- **Your name and logo** in the app your patients use, and your own address on steadyapp.co.za, such as `yourpractice.steadyapp.co.za`.
- **Two-step sign-in** for clinicians and administrators, from devices you've linked.
- **An access log** of every time a patient's information is opened. The master clinician and the practice administrator read it in the app.
- **Encrypted backups** every night, kept in South Africa.
- **Signed updates.** A new version installs on your server only if OpenWorks IT has signed it. The master clinician can install updates from the app.
- **Watched over.** Your server checks its own security and privacy every six hours. OpenWorks IT sees the results with each server's version, health and backups, never anything about patients.
- **An operator agreement** with OpenWorks IT, as POPIA requires, which the master clinician signs in the app.

[Privacy and security in detail](/security/).

## Support, training and the community

OpenWorks IT sets up your server, trains you and your colleagues, answers questions, and keeps Steady up to date. Changes that practices ask for go into Steady for everyone.

Every practice also joins the Steady community: a private forum of clinicians, opened from the Community tab in Steady with Steady's own sign-in, to talk about the science, build questionnaires together, plan studies across practices and shape what Steady does next. The Steady manual is there too, for your clinicians and your practice administrator. No patient information is ever posted there. [About the community](/community/).

There are two published plans, one for a solo clinician and one for a practice of up to three, and a quote for larger practices. [Ask us for current prices](mailto:info@openworks.co.za?subject=Steady%20prices). Patients never pay anything.

## Questions practices ask

**Do patients pay?** No. The app is free in the App Store and on Google Play.

**Which phones does it work on?** iPhones and Android phones. Watch data comes from Apple Health on iPhone and Health Connect on Android.

**Can I use it on a computer?** Yes. Clinicians and administrators can use Steady in a browser as well as on their phones, and patients without a phone can check in from a computer.

**Is it a chat app?** No. Patients can't message you through Steady, which keeps it from becoming an after-hours channel.

**Can I use my own domain?** No. Every practice's Steady has its own address on steadyapp.co.za, such as `yourpractice.steadyapp.co.za`: the community forum opens inside Steady only from those addresses, and we look after them, certificates and all. Your own website can link to yours.

**Can we host it ourselves?** Yes. The code and the setup guide are on [GitHub](https://github.com/OpenWorksIT/steady) under the GNU AGPL. Most practices would rather we did it.

**Who is it for?** Anyone in mental healthcare who sees people between sessions: psychologists, psychiatrists, counsellors, social workers, mental health nurses and occupational therapists. Steady calls them all clinicians. For a psychiatrist with a longer list of patients seen less often, between-visit check-ins may help most. Talk to us.
