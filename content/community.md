## Why a community

A single practice sees its own patients. Several practices, comparing notes, see much more: which questionnaires work in South African practice, which exercises patients actually do, what the patterns between sessions look like across hundreds of people rather than a handful.

Steady already gathers that kind of information inside each practice. The community is where clinicians turn it into shared knowledge, without patients' information ever leaving their own practice.

It opened in November 2026 with VR Clinical Psychology's clinicians, and grows with every practice that joins. Kempie van Rooyen leads it.

## What happens there

- **Clinical practice.** Using check-ins and flags well, fitting Steady into therapy, what works and what doesn't.
- **Research and studies.** Papers worth reading, and planning studies that run across several practices.
- **Questionnaires and tools.** Writing and testing new questionnaires, exercises and score bands together, and sorting out their licences. What the community builds can go into Steady for everyone.
- **The science.** How each part of Steady works and why, with the references.
- **The Steady manual.** How to use Steady, with screenshots. Practice administrators read it too, and it's where they ask their questions.
- **Steady's development.** Ideas, problems and what comes next, discussed directly with OpenWorks IT, who build it.
- **The tea room**, for introductions, and the work and life around it.
- **A private space per practice**, if a practice wants one, for its own clinicians only.

## Studies across practices

When practices run a study together, each one keeps its patients' information on its own server. Only totals and averages are shared, never individual records, and only from patients who have agreed to take part.

## The rules

These hold for everyone, the administrators included.

1. **Clinicians only, inside Steady.** The forum has no sign-up and no accounts of its own, and it doesn't open on its own in a browser. Everyone comes in through the Community tab, as who they are in Steady. Practice administrators come in for the Steady manual only.
2. **Steady's own sign-in for everyone**: a password, a code from an authenticator app and a linked device, at the person's own practice. No forum passwords, no emailed sign-in links, and no signing in with Google, Facebook or any other outside account.
3. **No patient-identifying information, ever.** No names or initials, no dates of birth, ID or file numbers, no screenshots of patient screens or notes, and no case details that together could point to one person. Talk about patterns, methods and made-up examples.
4. **Study data only as totals.** Never individual records.

If something slips through, an administrator deletes it for good, edit history included.

## What OpenWorks IT sees

OpenWorks IT hosts the forum, so we've been careful about our own access. Our accounts see four areas only: **Announcements**, **the Steady manual**, **the science** and **Steady development**. In the clinical areas, the tea room and any practice's own space we see only numbers, such as how many members, topics and posts there are, so we know the community is healthy. We never see their titles, posts or who wrote what.

Kempie, as the community's lead, holds the administrator role. Like any hosting company, OpenWorks IT can technically reach the server it runs; we use that access only to keep the forum running.

## Who can join

Clinicians at practices that use Steady. Each practice's master clinician chooses who from the practice comes in, in Steady, and OpenWorks IT lets them in. One email address belongs to one practice. Access is included in every practice's monthly fee. Clinicians who run Steady on their own servers can join for a separate fee.

Clinicians open it from the Community tab in Steady, on their phone or in a browser, already signed in. Patients have no Community tab.
